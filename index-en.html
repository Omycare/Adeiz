🟠 Important Points: Priority Fixes

6 POINTS · WEEK 1-2
C4 SLA Automations: workaround to replace with native conditions ↗ Automations
This group of SLA automations deserves to be reviewed. The 4 automations (urgent/high/normal/low) use a condition "Hours elapsed since creation (calendar) > 0" instead of Zendesk's native SLA conditions. This workaround is fragile.

There are specific conditions for triggering actions before or after an SLA breach — this is precisely one of the objectives of Zendesk's native SLA configuration. With these automations, part of the native SLA behavior is replaced when it would be simpler and more effective to rely directly on the built-in features designed for this.

Action: Review the full SLA automation group. Use native SLA conditions rather than a calendar time calculation from creation.
C5 2 Identical SLA Policies: one general policy is enough ↗ SLA
You have configured distinct SLA policies for each organization, when the conditions are exactly the same for all. I therefore recommend creating a general SLA policy applicable to all organizations, then adding specific exceptions if needed. This would simplify the configuration and make it easier to maintain.

Action: Create a single general SLA policy covering all organizations. Add specific exceptions only if SLAs differ by organization.
C6 SPF warning on the Gmail connector + sécurité email
An SPF alert is present on the ADEIZ Gmail connector. Without properly configured SPF, emails sent from Zendesk may be marked as spam by recipients, impacting the deliverability of notifications and follow-ups.
Action: Configure the SPF record to include Zendesk servers (add include:mail.zendesk.com to DNS). Also verify DKIM and DMARC.
C7 Duplicate "Quick Assignment" Trigger + Ultimate.ai Residual ↗ Triggers
  • The "Quick Assignment" trigger exists as two active copies (IDs 28970987661340 and another): possible conflict in automatic routing

Action: Identify and remove the duplicate.
C8 Explore "Top 10 Sub-categories" Report Broken ↗ Explore
The "Top 10 sub-categories" report uses custom attributes that need to be reconfigured to exclude null values: this approach does not work in Zendesk Explore and the report returns no results. Additionally, native Zendesk reports cannot be modified directly. To work with them, it is recommended to create clones (master clones) on which to apply customizations.
Action: Clone the native reports. Reconfigure the sub-category custom attribute with the correct null-exclusion method.

💡 Opportunités & Flux recommandés

QUICK WINS + LONG TERM
💡 Public Help Center for Stores
Why this opportunity exists
The volume of tickets on recurring and documentable topics is high: Checkout (318 tickets), PosCloud, extranet access, startup procedures. These requests come mainly by phone, the usual channel for stores with ADEIZ. The opportunity is not to change the channel, but to allow store teams to find answers themselves for the simplest cases, before needing to call.

The content is already there
ADEIZ has 917 articles covering common store issues: register troubleshooting (Checkout: 318 tickets identified), PosCloud, extranet access, opening procedures. These articles exist but are only accessible to internal agents. Opening a selection as self-service does not require creating content: it is about making what already exists visible, structuring it and securing it by segment (Armand Thiery, Printemps organizations, etc.).

Expected Impact
Making the HC accessible as self-service on recurring and documentable topics (Checkout, basic network, PosCloud, startup procedures) allows store teams to resolve issues themselves before calling. According to industry benchmarks, a well-structured self-service HC can deflect 15 to 25% of N1 volume. For ADEIZ, this represents approximately 120 to 200 fewer tickets/year on problems whose answers already exist in writing.

Recommendation
Identify the 20 to 30 articles most consulted by agents on recurring tickets and open them with restricted public access by organization. Structure a "Store Help" category distinct from the internal category.
💡 Automatic Escalation for Near-Breached SLAs
SLA alert automations are well configured (8/15/30/60 min by priority) but only alert the assigned agent. Adding an automatic escalation to the N2 group if the SLA is still at risk 5 minutes after the alert would prevent breaches.
💡 Dedicated Maintainer Explore Dashboard
With 4 active maintainers (Diebold, Stackr, Nedap, Orange) and differentiated escalation workflows, a dedicated Explore report (response time by maintainer, resolution rate, missing external refs) would provide essential visibility for contract negotiations.
💡 Maintainer Flow: To Revise with Native Zendesk Tools
The maintainer flow exists and covers the 4 partners (Diebold, Stackr, Nedap, Orange) with escalation triggers, follow-ups and dedicated statuses. However, the current logic is complex and deserves to be revisited.

There are two Zendesk features that could better address this complexity:
Workflow Actions Native Feature
Native automation for conditional actions on tickets. Simpler and more reliable than combined triggers.
Advanced Workflow Actions Native Feature
Extended version, suited for complex multi-actor escalations like the current maintainer workflow (Diebold, Stackr, Nedap, Orange).

These tools would simplify configuration, reduce the risk of trigger conflicts and facilitate maintenance.
💡 Recommended Apps
Attachment Plus: advanced attachment management (preview, bulk download) useful for tickets with technical diagrams and hardware screenshots.

Advanced Search: advanced multi-criteria search in tickets. Very useful for N2 agents managing complex escalations with combined filters (organization + maintainer + status + tag).
